ABOUT MA'AN ACADEMY

Learning Arabic. Growing Together.

A meaningful approach to Arabic learning, inspired by Montessori education, immersion, and a deep appreciation for the Arabic language.

01

Who Are We?

Ma'an Academy is a non-profit institution/academy that aims to help everyone who wants to learn Arabic in an ingenious and more interesting way.

At Ma’an Academy, we use the Montessori approach to teach children the Arabic language. Montessori is a method of education based on self-directed activity, hands-on learning, and collaborative play.

In Montessori classrooms, children make creative choices in their learning, while the classroom environment and highly trained teachers offer age-appropriate activities to guide the process.

Ma'an Academy was established by a group of educators and parents who value the importance of the Arabic language.

02

Why Establish Ma'an?

Our main purpose in establishing Ma’an Academy was to provide the community with a rich Arabic program that is taught in an interactive, fun, and easy way using Maria Montessori’s approach.

The Montessori approach supports us in instilling a love of learning the Arabic language in our next generation.

Loving the Arabic language and deeply understanding it will allow our children to better understand the Holy Quran.

At Ma’an Academy, we believe that languages are learned best through immersion.

Learning by immersion is one of the most natural ways humans learn. Our students will therefore be fully immersed in the Arabic language during their time at the Academy.

This approach can lead to faster learning, deeper understanding of the Arabic language, and a greater appreciation for the language.

“To learn a language, the child must live with those who speak it, otherwise he will not be able to.” — Maria Montessori
OUR ACADEMIC APPROACH

How We Help Students Learn

Our approach combines Arabic immersion, Montessori-inspired learning, hands-on activities, and meaningful interaction to create a learning experience children can enjoy and remember.

01

Montessori-Inspired

Children are encouraged to explore, make choices, participate actively, and develop independence throughout their learning journey.

02

Arabic Immersion

Students experience Arabic naturally through communication, activities, interaction, and meaningful everyday learning experiences.

03

Hands-On Learning

Learning becomes more engaging through activities that allow students to interact with ideas rather than simply memorize information.

04

Love of Learning

We aim to create positive experiences that help students develop confidence, curiosity, and a lasting connection with the Arabic language.
